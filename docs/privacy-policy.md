# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

**Spend Like a Millionaire** ("the app") is an entertainment app that works offline. This policy
explains what information the app handles.

## What stays on your device

Your language, currency, selected person and shopping cart are stored only on your device so the
app remembers them. This information is never sent to us and we do not have accounts or logins.

## Information collected automatically

When the app is built with Firebase services enabled, the following is collected by Google on our
behalf to keep the app stable and to update small pieces of content:

- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** crash logs, device model, operating system version,
  app version and an app-instance identifier.
- **Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics):** basic, aggregated events about how the app is used
  (for example screens opened), device information and an app-instance identifier.
- **Remote Config:** the app downloads small configuration values (for example updated net-worth
  figures). The request includes standard network information such as your IP address.

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos or any other
personal content. We do not sell personal information.

## Advertising

The app does not currently show ads. If advertising (Google AdMob) is added in a future version,
this policy will be updated before release, and where required you will be asked for consent
(for example in the European Economic Area and the UK).

## Sharing the receipt

If you tap "Share receipt", the app creates an image on your device and opens the Android share
sheet. Where the image goes is decided by the app you choose to share it with.

## Children

The app is a general-audience entertainment app and is not directed at children under 13.

## Third-party services

Google Firebase: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## Your choices

You can clear all locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it in Android
settings. To limit analytics, you can reset your Android advertising identifier or disable usage
and diagnostics sharing in your device settings.
